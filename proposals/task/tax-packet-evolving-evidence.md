## 2. User persona and prior history

Who is the user? Briefly describe the background, preferences and prior experiences that matter to this task.

**✍️ Your answer (required):** Kai (fictional) changed jobs during 2025, moving from Atlas Labs to Brook Systems. Kai holds two brokerage accounts (Northfield Brokerage and Ridgeway Invest) and a checking account at Cedar Bank, and closed a savings account at Harbor Bank in 2025. Kai has already completed a personal-tax intake with their accountant, Morgan Lee, and agreed to deliver supporting records by `2026-03-10`, a date set between Kai and Morgan, not a legal deadline. Morgan decides filing status, residency, deductions and tax owed.

Before the task starts, the tax workspace holds the 2024 Northfield statement and the 2024 organizer, kept for reference. Kai's mailbox holds tax-form emails from issuers and a CPA instruction sheet listing the fields Morgan wants: W-2 box 1/2, 1099-INT box 1, 1099-DIV box 1a/1b listed separately (1b is part of 1a, never added to it), and 1099-B proceeds and reported basis as printed. Kai wants original records left untouched and says what they still need to confirm, but otherwise expects the agent to keep the packet current without being told what changed.

## 3. Story outline

What does the user want, what changes, and what would a good outcome look like? Mention the approximate time span if relevant.

**✍️ Your answer (required):** Kai wants one tax packet that the agent maintains across five check-ins, then uploads to Morgan's workspace `CP-2025-KAI` once authorized. The story runs from February 3 to March 10, 2026, Los Angeles time (`America/Los_Angeles`, UTC offset `-08:00`; S5 falls after the March 8 DST change, so its offset is `-07:00`).

- **S1 `2026-02-03T09:00:00-08:00`:** Kai gives the initial request (intake scope: Atlas, Brook, Northfield, Ridgeway, Cedar; read-only access; no upload until authorized; no filing or payment). The Atlas W-2 and Cedar 1099-INT are available, along with the 2024 Northfield statement and the 2024 organizer. The agent should build a five-source inventory, mark Brook, Northfield and Ridgeway as missing, and treat 2024 files as reference only.
- **S2 `2026-02-18T10:00:00-08:00`:** *"This batch arrived; keep organizing and prepare the handoff draft."* The Brook W-2 arrives, plus Northfield's original 2025 consolidated statement and a re-exported copy of the same statement with a different hash. Ridgeway's consolidated statement arrives with a supplemental transaction view that repeats the same transaction IDs. The agent must save a real manifest, a field-level summary and a handoff draft, with no double counting.
- **S3 `2026-03-02T09:30:00-08:00`:** *"Take a look at the new tax mail and keep things updated."* Northfield sends a CORRECTED consolidated statement whose notice says it replaces the original in full. The agent should adopt it and update every saved current artifact, without being told what changed; the original stays in an internal audit area.
- **S4 `2026-03-06T18:00:00-08:00`:** *"Harbor just sent a tax-form notice for the savings account I closed last year. Include it. With Harbor added, I confirm there are no other sources for this intake."* The Harbor notice says 2025 interest was paid and the 1099-INT will be downloadable on March 10. Separately, a newly forwarded email carries the original Northfield statement again. The agent should add a sixth, missing source and keep the correction in place despite the later timestamp. It must not call the packet complete.
- **S5 `2026-03-10T10:00:00-07:00`:** *"It's delivery day. Please finish up. If everything is in, upload to Morgan's CP-2025-KAI; if something blocks it, tell me what's missing."* The Harbor 1099-INT is now in the account's document page. The agent retrieves it, re-verifies all six sources and derived files, uploads the current packet, reads the portal receipt, and reports which of packet ready, packet delivered and return filed (always false) are true.

A good outcome has six current documents in the delivered packet (both W-2s, corrected Northfield, Ridgeway, Cedar, Harbor) plus a manifest, a source-linked summary and a handoff note that agree with each other and with the portal receipt. The final totals are wages 100,000.00; withholding 12,000.00; interest 375.00; dividends 1a 800.00 and 1b 600.00 listed separately; proceeds 20,000.00; and reported basis 19,300.00. Superseded, duplicate and prior-year files stay out of the current set.

## 4. What makes this distinct and personal

How does this person's context change the right action, and what might a generic assistant miss? Use one concrete example to show the request or trigger, where and when the agent can discover the decisive personal context, and how that context changes the appropriate action. If relevant, include one key change and the user’s response or non-response.

**✍️ Your answer (required):** The right packet depends on Kai's own history, not on what a typical filer receives. A generic assistant tends to keep only the current employer's W-2, treat a closed account as irrelevant, or trust the newest email. Here the correct state comes from facts the agent has to carry forward from earlier sessions: Kai's job change, the intake scope, the correction notice, and Kai's later addition of Harbor and confirmation that the source list is complete.

For example, on March 6 the agent sees two things at once. Kai adds the closed Harbor account and confirms the scope. A forwarded email, newer than the March 2 correction, re-attaches the original Northfield statement. The agent already knows from S3 that Northfield replaced that statement in full, so the newer email must not roll back the summary (DIV 1a 800 → 760, basis 19,300 → 18,500). The new source must also reopen completeness: "all five known sources are in", which was true on February 18, is no longer the final state, and the 300.00 interest is now a verified subtotal, not the year total. On March 10, Kai's conditional authorization ("if everything is in, upload") only takes effect once Harbor's document has actually been retrieved and the packet re-verified.

The hard part is propagation. Saying "I used the corrected statement" in chat is not enough if the saved summary, manifest or upload package still holds February values.
